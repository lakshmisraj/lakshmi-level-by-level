Lakshmi,
Level by Level

Eight chapters, from a crooked school tie at three to late nights, hackathons and the D train in Brooklyn. Walk around, open every memory, and grow up one chapter at a time.

←↑↓→ or WASD walk E / Space look, talk, continue M memory book The score is original chiptune in the style of each era's music. Sparkles mark things you haven't looked at yet. Doors open once a chapter's main moments are done.
